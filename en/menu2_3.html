beau;leaf
Unmanned Water Quality Monitoring Robot, beau;leaf
Introducing beau;leaf, an unmanned
water quality monitoring robot.
  • In Collaboration with Korea Water Resources Corporation (K-Water)
  • Patented Unmanned Water Quality Robot & Control System
  • Patented Autonomous Charging System
  • Patent Pending for Amphibious Water Surveillance Robot
  • 01
    Amphibious Robot
    Operates on both land and water.
    Supports autonomous charging outside the water.
  • 02
    Water Quality Data Collection
    Equipped with sensors to measure temperature,
    pH, turbidity, chlorophyll-a, and more.
  • 03
    Monitoring System
    Transmits data via LoRa and LTE to a web-based system.
    Analyzes collected data to assess water quality risk levels.

beau;leaf in Action
Various real-world applications of beau;leaf
Function 1
Oil Fence Deployment
Deploys an oil fence barrier to contain
oil spills and minimize environmental damage.
Function 2
Water Quality Monitoring System
Transmits collected water quality data in real-time.
Integrated into ETRI and K-Water’s environmental
database system.
Function 3
River & Ocean Water Quality Testing
Conducts real-time water quality testing without
human intervention.Provides immediate alerts
for pollution or contamination.
Function 4
Nighttime Decorative Lighting
Serves as a corporate mascot display and ambient
lighting, adding an entertainment element.
