CEO’s Message
Robots Designed for People,
With Only What’s Truly Needed

At The Robotics, we lead innovation in mobility robotics by integrating
appropriate convergence technologies. Our mission is to enhance human
lives and develop sustainable technologies that protect the environment.

We carefully consider the impact of technology on both people and nature,
continuously striving to fulfill our responsibilities. Through relentless research and
innovation, we have developed practical solutions that genuinely meet people’s needs,
focusing on mobility robots that play a crucial role in agriculture and industrial fields.

Every project and product at The Robotics is founded on two core values: human-centered
design and environmental sustainability. By upholding these principles, we contribute to
building a safer, more efficient, and more sustainable future.

Our vision is to create a world where people can live with dignity,
and the environment remains preserved for future generations.We will continue to take on new challenges and create new value,
moving forward toward a better future.


Kang Dong-Woo,
CEO of The Robotics Co., Ltd.
